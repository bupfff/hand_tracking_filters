Portal filters

Hold up both hands with your index fingers and thumbs out. A portal opens between them.

  • Spread for more. Put out more fingers and every gap between matching fingers becomes its own portal, each with the next filter along. Curl them back in for one portal.
  • Pinch to switch. Bring all four fingertips together in the middle. With more fingers out, just touch your fingertips together.
  • Swipe to go back. Flick the portal right for the previous filter, left for the next. The arrow buttons work too.
  • Tilt to tune. Raise one hand above the other to dial the filter up or down.
  • Fist to stamp. Hold a still fist to pin a copy of the portal in place. The live one keeps following your hands, so you can stamp a few around the room. Tap the banner or press C to clear them, or Backspace to remove the last one.
